## About Me

### A little introduction

Hello, and welcome! My name is Harsh Vardhan. I am a student at Manas International School, and I live in Bahadurpur, Laheriasarai, in the state of Bihar. This website is my small corner of the internet, where I get to introduce myself in my own words.

## My Thoughts

### On learning

I think learning never really ends. Marks and exams are important, but the real goal of studying is to understand things well enough that they stay with us for life. When I take the time to understand a topic instead of just memorising it, I feel more confident and I remember it for longer.

### On hard work

Talent can give a person a start, but hard work is what carries them forward. Big goals are reached through small efforts repeated every day. Some days the work feels slow, but I try to remind myself that slow progress is still progress.

### On kindness and respect

I believe that how we treat people says more about us than what we own or what we score. Being polite, honest and helpful costs nothing, yet it can make someone’s whole day better. I want to be a person others can trust.

### On time

Time is the one thing we cannot get back once it is gone. I am learning to use it wisely: to study when it is time to study, to rest when it is time to rest, and to spend some of it with the people I love.
